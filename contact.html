02 Before you write

Questions we're usually asked first

How do you price work?

Scope, timeline, and transparent pricing — fixed or retainer. You see the estimate before the build starts, and scope changes get an honest cost rather than a quiet one.

How will I know where the project stands?

Weekly demos of working software, plus full access to boards and repos. Progress is something you watch run, not something you read about.

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes. Alongside full product builds we run retainer and team-augmentation engagements, with senior engineers embedded in your team and time-zone overlap for US clients.

What happens after launch?

Production hardening, monitoring and go-live support, then SLAs, enhancements and on-call. We stand by our work after the release, not just up to it.

What technologies do you build on?

React, Next.js, TypeScript and Flutter on the front end; .NET Core and Node.js with SQL Server, PostgreSQL, Dapper and EF Core behind them; Azure, Docker and GitHub Actions for delivery. We pick what fits your goals rather than defaulting to one stack.
